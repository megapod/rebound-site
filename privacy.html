Privacy Policy — Rebound

App: Rebound (package com.yehuda.rebound) · Developer: Yehuda P. · Contact: yehuda.g.p@gmail.com · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

Rebound is a basketball game you can play alone or online. This policy explains what data is processed when you play, and by whom. The short version: you can play without any account, and the game never asks for your e-mail or phone number. Google services power sign-in, ads, usage statistics and purchases. Our own game server runs online play while you play, and receives anonymous statistics about how the game is played. We never sell data.

Data stored on your device only

Settings, progress, credits and the time of the daily reminder are saved on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad-interaction events. This processing is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from ads

Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), you will be shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are served, and you may choose non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used in general: how many people open it, how often they come back, and how long a session lasts. For this purpose Google collects and processes data such as an app-instance identifier, your device's advertising ID, device model and operating-system version, country derived from IP address, and usage events (for example, first open and session start). The game does not send your name, email address, or anything you type.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and security in Firebase

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases (such as a stage pass that removes ads for a stage) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase records are managed by your Google account. Refunds are handled according to Google Play's refund policy.

Sign-in (Google Play Games and Firebase Authentication)

When the game starts, it signs you in to Google Play Games silently, if your phone is signed in to it. For online play the game exchanges that sign-in for a Firebase Authentication token (by Google), and our server checks the token so nobody can play online as someone else. Through them we receive your Play Games player name and an account identifier. Google keeps a sign-in record for us in Firebase Authentication. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Online play

Our game server (a "relay", run by us on our own computer and on rented machines at Oracle Cloud and Google Cloud) finds you an opponent, opens rooms by code, and passes the moves between the two players. The moves and the state of a game are held only in memory while it is open. The server sees your IP address while you are connected, to deliver the game's messages and to limit abuse. The server's logs, which can include the IP address and the account identifier of a connection, are kept only for a short time and then overwritten.

Playing on the same Wi-Fi

Two phones on the same Wi-Fi network can play each other directly. The game finds the other phone on that network; these messages stay on the local network and do not reach our server.

Anonymous game statistics

The game makes a random number on your phone. It is not your name, your account, your advertising ID or your phone's serial number, and a new one is made if you reinstall the game. While the game is open, it tells our server that it is here (with that number, the game's version, and which screen and mode are open) and receives the game's settings, including which test group your phone belongs to. Once a day (with the day the game was first opened), and when certain things happen in the game (a credit is earned or used, a game starts or ends, a rewarded ad is offered or shown, a step of the tutorial, the daily reminder), the game sends a short note with that number and a few short details (for example the game mode, the score, the number of credits, or why an ad did not load). If your phone is one of Google's automated test devices, the note says so. These messages are encrypted on your phone so that only our server can read them. We use them to see how the game is played, and we cannot tie them to you. They are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted.

Daily reminder

If you allow notifications, the game sets one reminder a day on your phone. It is made by your phone, not sent from our server.

Files downloaded from our site

The game downloads two small files from our site, which is hosted on GitHub Pages: the list of our game servers, and a file that says whether a newer version exists. As with any website, the host receives your IP address. No other information about you is sent.

Children and teenagers

Rebound is not directed at children under 13. We do not ask for anyone's name, e-mail or phone number. If you are a parent and believe your child under 13 has used the game's online features, write to yehuda.g.p@gmail.com and we will delete what our server holds for that player.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with any third party. The Google services and the site hosting described above receive the data listed in their sections and operate under their own policies.

Data retention and deletion

Our server keeps no player profile for Rebound: no names, friends or tokens are stored. It keeps only which test group each random number belongs to, so that the same phone stays in the same group, for up to 90 days. Anonymous statistics are kept for up to 90 days. The sign-in record at Firebase Authentication is deleted after 90 days without a sign-in, or earlier on request. To request deletion, send an e-mail to yehuda.g.p@gmail.com with the subject "Rebound - delete my data" and your Play Games player name. We delete within 30 days and confirm by e-mail. Usage statistics are held by Google Analytics, in aggregated or pseudonymous form, for a limited period set by that service. Local data is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Changes to this policy

When the game starts processing new data, this page is updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date changes.
